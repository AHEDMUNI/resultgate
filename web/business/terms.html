Commission

The commercial model is a 5% provider commission on each eligible successful paid transaction attributed to ResultGate, counted once. No joining fee, monthly ResultGate subscription or separate customer recommendation fee applies. Commission is triggered by processor-confirmed payment, not service completion or a quality check. Unpaid orders, visits and clicks do not generate commission.

The eligible base is the service subtotal after discounts and excluding separately identified taxes and tips. Commission is rounded to the nearest currency minor unit, with half units rounded up. Refunds proportionately reduce commission and full refunds reverse it. Disputed amounts suspend the associated entitlement pending resolution; a successful defence may restore it, while a lost dispute reverses it.

The provider allocation is 95% before processing fees and other agreed adjustments. Fees, payout timing, reserves and responsibility for disputes must be specified in the provider/payment agreement. No immediate payout or fixed net receipt is guaranteed.
