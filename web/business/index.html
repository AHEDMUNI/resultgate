WHAT THE MARKETPLACE IS FOR

Specialist digital services, with a defined scope.

Public-data processing

Cleaning, formatting and analysing data that a customer is permitted to use. Deliverables may include structured tables or a written analysis.

Document & format conversion

Turning permitted content into useful formats, such as structured text or machine-readable records.

Agent & API workflows

Scoped technical tasks offered by providers who own their services or hold the required permission to offer them.

These describe intended service categories, not currently purchasable listings. Each future listing must identify its provider, scope, price in USD, delivery method, expected timing and cancellation terms before checkout.

ONE TRANSPARENT PLATFORM COMMISSION

15% on eligible paid transactions.

No provider joining fee. No ResultGate monthly subscription. No separate recommendation fee for customers.

The platform commission becomes due when the payment processor confirms successful payment. Service completion or a quality audit is not a condition for the commission. Refunds and disputes can reduce or reverse it.

The provider’s allocation is 85% before payment-processing fees and other agreed adjustments. The final allocation of those costs must be disclosed in the agreement.
