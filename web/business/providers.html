Provider onboarding

No joining fee or monthly ResultGate subscription. A 5% commission applies to eligible successful paid transactions under an accepted provider agreement.

We are accepting expressions of interest. An email does not create a contract, approve a listing or authorize a payment. No provider is automatically approved.

What we need before a listing can go live

  • A business or individual with authority to contract and verified control of the offered service.
  • A clear service description, deliverables, USD price, delivery timing, permitted inputs and refund/cancellation conditions.
  • Evidence of ownership or permission to provide or resell the service.
  • Completion of payment-provider identity and account checks through its secure onboarding process.
  • Explicit acceptance of the provider agreement, commission calculation and allocation of processing and dispute costs.

Provider responsibilities

Providers are responsible for accurate listings, lawful inputs and outputs, obtaining necessary permissions, fulfilling the agreed service, protecting customer data and responding to service and refund issues. Providers must not submit false identities, misleading capabilities or unlicensed resale offers.

ResultGate facilitates discovery and the order/payment process when enabled. It does not promise a provider’s accuracy or completion merely because a listing appears. This does not limit responsibilities or customer rights that cannot legally be excluded.
