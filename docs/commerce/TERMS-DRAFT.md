# Provider terms for review — not signed or legally approved

- No ResultGate joining fee or monthly subscription.
- ResultGate commission: 5% of the eligible paid service subtotal (USD initial release),
  excluding separately identified taxes/tips and adjusted for discounts/refunds.
- Entitlement starts upon processor-confirmed successful payment. No service completion,
  quality inspection, click or unpaid order establishes or delays commission.
- Each underlying payment is counted once; recurring payments need a separate supported
  agreement and are not part of this pilot.
- Provider share is 95% before processor fees and other agreed adjustments. This does
  not guarantee immediate availability, payout date or 95% net cash.
- Partial refunds proportionately reduce the commission; full refunds reverse it.
  Disputes suspend entitlement; a won dispute restores applicable commission, and a
  lost dispute reverses it. Recoveries and charges must be reconciled through Stripe.
- Processor fees, dispute costs, negative balances, taxes and responsibility for
  customer refunds require explicit allocation in the final agreement. They are not
  silently added to the 5% commission or hidden from the provider/customer.
- Provider must demonstrate authority to contract, service ownership or resale rights,
  and complete required Stripe onboarding. Public APIs are not automatic resale rights.
- Service price, scope, provider identity, cancellation/refund terms and commission
  disclosure must be visible before customer approval. No requester recommendation fee.

Acceptance evidence design: authenticated representative, provider identity, terms
version/hash, explicit acceptance timestamp and independent authority-review record.
The implementation records SANDBOX declarations only. Production identity verification
and binding agreement execution are pending human/legal review; no agreement is accepted
on AHED MUNI's behalf. No live provider approval is represented by sandbox fixtures.
