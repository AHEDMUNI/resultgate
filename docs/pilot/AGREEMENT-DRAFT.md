During the technical pilot all commission tests are explicitly **simulation**, no debt demand, invoice or payment. Moving to binding commercial eligibility requires a separately approved complete agreement, evidence integration, maturity/refund rules and owner authorization. The current code supports USD fixed micro-units or basis points and one outcome per referral; other currencies, renewal schedules, tax treatment and settlement are not implemented. If desired commercial terms exceed those limits, do not activate the agreement in the pilot.

Review checklist: both parties understand scope and can decline; no guaranteed referrals or earnings; no authority to accept customer jobs without consent; no automatic agreement from registration; no guarantee a registered provider will become executable; all blanks and interpretation questions resolved before any separate signing. ResultGate's assistant is not authorized to sign for either party.
